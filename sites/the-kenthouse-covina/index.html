A few honest words first

Reaching out is the hard part. Once you do, you will not be doing this alone.

Mental health hits close to home for most of us, and it has for me too. That is why care here begins with a real conversation, not a checklist. I want to understand what you are carrying, what you have already tried and what a better week would actually look like for you.

From there we build a plan together. For some people that means medication, managed carefully and reviewed often. For others it is talk therapy, lifestyle changes or some combination of all three. Whatever it is, it should make sense to you and move you toward the life you are working for.

Common questions

Good to know before you book

Do you prescribe medication?

Yes. As a psychiatric nurse practitioner I evaluate, diagnose and prescribe for a wide range of mental health conditions, and I manage and adjust medications over time. Many clients combine medication with supportive therapy.

Which insurance do you take?

The Kenthouse is in network with Aetna and Optum. Self-pay is welcome if you are out of network, and a superbill is available so you can seek reimbursement from your plan.

Can we meet by video instead of in person?

Absolutely. Secure telehealth visits are available to clients anywhere in California, and you are welcome to mix video and in-person appointments depending on your week.

What happens in the first appointment?

The first visit is a longer conversation, usually 50 to 60 minutes, about your history, what you are experiencing now and what you would like to change. From there we build a plan together. Nothing is decided for you.

How soon can I be seen?

New clients are usually seen within a week. Reach out with a couple of times that work for you and we will find a spot, in Covina or by telehealth.
