The starting point

A full picture before any decisions

Most care at The Kenthouse begins here. The evaluation is a calm, thorough conversation about your history, your symptoms and your goals, so that whatever comes next is built on a clear understanding of you, not a rushed first impression.

Request an appointment Call (951) 404-0701

What it is

An hour to be properly understood

The first visit usually runs 50 to 60 minutes. We talk through what you are experiencing now, how long it has been going on, what you have already tried and what you would like to feel different. If it helps to bring notes or a family member, you are welcome to.

By the end you will have a clear, plain-language explanation of what is likely going on and an honest look at the options, from medication to therapy to simply checking back in. You decide what happens next, with my guidance.

What it covers

Thorough, but never clinical to a fault

Your history

A look at your mental health, medical background and anything in your life that is shaping how you feel right now.

Current symptoms

A careful read of mood, sleep, focus, anxiety and anything else that has been getting in the way of daily life.

A working diagnosis

An honest, understandable explanation of what is most likely going on, without jargon or labels for their own sake.

Your goals

What a better week actually looks like for you. Treatment is measured against your goals, not a generic checklist.

Treatment options

A clear menu of next steps, with the benefits and trade-offs of each laid out so you can choose with confidence.

A plan to start

You leave with a first step and a sense of the road ahead, plus a follow-up booked so nothing stalls.

Ready when you are

Book your evaluation in Covina or by telehealth. New clients are usually seen within a week, and I am happy to answer questions before you commit to anything.
