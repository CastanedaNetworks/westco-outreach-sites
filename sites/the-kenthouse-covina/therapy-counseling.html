Talk therapy that gives you something to use

Sometimes the most useful thing is a steady, judgment-free space and a few practical tools. Supportive counseling at The Kenthouse draws on proven approaches and meets you where you are, whether you come for therapy alone or alongside medication.

What it feels like

Heard first, then helped

Sessions are conversations, not interrogations. We start with what is on your mind, then work toward understanding the patterns underneath it and what might shift them. You set the pace and the priorities.

Because the same provider handles your therapy and, if needed, your medication, nothing gets lost between offices. Your full picture stays in one place, with one person who knows your story.

Approaches I draw on

Evidence-based, never one-size-fits-all

Therapy is tailored to you. These are the methods I lean on most, often blended to fit the moment.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Practical work on the thoughts and behaviors that keep anxiety, depression and stress in motion, with skills you can use between sessions.

Motivational Interviewing

A collaborative way to work through ambivalence and find your own reasons for change, especially when a habit or decision feels stuck.

Solution-Focused Work

Short, goal-oriented sessions that build on what is already working in your life and move you toward concrete next steps.

Supportive Counseling

A consistent, encouraging space during hard stretches, grief or major life change, where you can think out loud without being rushed.
