01From the editor

A new salon in the LC Valley

A new salon on Thain Road.

Whitney Barnes is opening a new salon at 247 Thain Road, Suite 107 in Lewiston, Idaho. Custom color, extensions, and the kind of long-form appointments that don't survive in a busy walk-in shop.

The Instagram, @red.hair.salon, has three posts and one hundred fifty-two followers as of this writing. The third post is the wordmark on leopard print — a bold red ‘RED' beside a flourish of script. The first is Whitney standing in red scrubs in an empty suite. The second is the room from another angle, drywall still raw. That's the whole archive. Everything else is yet to happen.

Soft opening is on the calendar for 2026. Until then, slots are reserved by DM.

02A list of services

The menu

Four services. Booked direct.

  1. No. 01 Custom color Single process, dimensional, gloss work, and corrections. Consultation included — you and Whitney decide what's worth doing before the bowl comes out. Consult
  2. No. 02 Extensions Length, density, fill-ins. Color-matched at install. Follow-ups scheduled at the chair so the spot doesn't go to the calendar. Consult
  3. No. 03 Cut + finish Wash, cut, blowout. Built around the color or extension work — not a rush job between two bigger appointments. From $
  4. No. 04 Standing slot Recurring maintenance for color and extension clients. Six-week, eight-week, twelve-week — pick one and keep it. Recurring
03The stylist

Behind the chair

Whitney Barnes.

By the stylist

Whitney has cut, colored, and styled in the Lewis–Clark Valley for years before opening RED. The salon is built around long-form appointments — custom color and extensions take the time they take, and the chairs are booked accordingly.

The bio is short on purpose. Two specialties — custom color and extensions — and time enough to do both correctly. Consultations are part of the booking, not a separate appointment.

The Instagram is where the buildout, the soft-opening dates, and the booking conversation live. Posts will get less sparse as the doors get closer.
