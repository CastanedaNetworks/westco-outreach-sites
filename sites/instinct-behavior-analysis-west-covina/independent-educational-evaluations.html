For families & districts

An independent read on what your child needs.

When a family and a district see an evaluation differently, federal law provides a way forward: an independent educational evaluation by a qualified outside examiner. We conduct IEEs that are thorough, impartial and written to be used, not shelved, with assessments across behavior, psychology and speech and language.

What this covers

Inside an independent evaluation

A complete IEE draws on the assessment domains the referral question requires.

Functional behavior assessments

Target behaviors, ABC analysis, the function behind the behavior and a behavior intervention plan built on what we find.

Functional ecological assessments

How home, school and community environments shape the student's behavior.

Psychological evaluations

Cognitive functioning, academic achievement, social-emotional functioning, attention and executive functioning.

Speech & language assessments

Receptive and expressive language, social communication, articulation, fluency and functional communication.

Records review & observation

The full file, plus direct observation in the settings where the behavior actually happens.

IEP meeting presentation

A plain-language report, presented to the team in person, with the hard questions answered.

Districts hire us too. Our findings follow the data, whoever is paying, which is exactly why the report carries weight at the table.

Know your rights

If you disagree with the district's evaluation of your child, you have the right under IDEA to request an independent educational evaluation, often at public expense. The district must either fund the IEE or file for due process to defend its own evaluation. Ask for the request requirements in writing.

Good fit

When an IEE is the right move

  • A family disagrees with the district's evaluation of their child.
  • A district wants a defensible outside opinion on a contested case.
  • A placement decision is in dispute and the team is stuck.
  • A behavior plan is not working and nobody agrees on why.
