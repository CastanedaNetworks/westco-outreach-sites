What this covers

Inside a school-based engagement

We staff and support school teams across general and special education settings.

Behavior Intervention Implementers (BII)

One-on-one support in the classroom: running the behavior plan, real-time redirection, transitions, peer engagement and data collection.

Behavior Intervention Developers (BID)

Intervention design and monitoring: developing behavior plans, analyzing data and supervising BII staff against IEP objectives.

BCBA clinical leadership

Board Certified Behavior Analysts conduct assessments, interpret data, train staff, support IEP meetings and keep implementation honest.

Registered Behavior Technicians (RBT)

Direct, in-class support implementing individualized strategies for behavior regulation and skill development.

Assessment & evaluation services

Functional behavior assessments, functional ecological assessments, behavioral and adaptive functioning evaluations and classroom observations.

IEP team consultation

We sit with the team, explain the data and help write goals that can be measured.

How it works

Measured at every step, like everything we do.

1

Referral & review

We start with the IEP, the data and a classroom observation.

2

Match & plan

The right level of support, from BII coverage to BCBA oversight, scoped to the student's plan.

3

Deliver in class

Staff in the room, supervised and supported, with data collected from day one.

4

Review against the IEP

Scheduled reviews confirm progress on IEP objectives or trigger a plan change.

Good fit

When schools bring us in

  • IEP minutes call for one-on-one behavior support the district cannot staff.
  • A behavior plan exists on paper but the classroom is not improving.
  • The FBA referral list is longer than the school psychologist's calendar.
  • Paraprofessionals and aides need practical behavior training fast.
