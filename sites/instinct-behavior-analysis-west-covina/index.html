How an engagement runs

The same discipline we teach is the discipline we work by.

1

Phase A

Baseline

Every engagement starts with measurement. We observe in the real setting, take data and establish exactly where things stand before anyone touches a plan.

2

Phase B

Intervention

We design the protocol, train your people on it and implement together, with fidelity checks along the way. A plan only works when it is actually followed.

3

Phase C

Generalization

Then we work ourselves out of a job. Skills carry across settings, staff and time, and the data shows it. That is what lasting impact means to us.

About the firm

A dedicated consulting firm that stands out for effectiveness and integrity.

Instinct Behavior Analysis was founded in 2019 by Ivan Montenegro and is headquartered in West Covina, California. The science of behavior is rigorous, and the way it gets delivered should be too. We keep our caseload deliberate, our recommendations honest and our data clean, whether we are training a new cohort of technicians, helping a founder open the doors to their own agency or sitting beside a family at an IEP meeting.
