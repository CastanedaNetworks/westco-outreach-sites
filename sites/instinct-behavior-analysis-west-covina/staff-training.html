What this covers

Inside a staff training engagement

Training engagements are built around your caseload and your gaps, drawing from these core areas.

New technician onboarding

A structured first-weeks curriculum that takes new hires from orientation to confident first sessions.

Competency-based skill checks

Hands-on assessments against defined performance criteria, not just quizzes.

Supervisor development

Training for mid-tier and senior staff on feedback, fidelity checks and case oversight.

In-session coaching

Side-by-side shadowing with immediate, specific feedback.

Documentation & data collection

Clean session notes and reliable data practices your auditors and funders will thank you for.

Refresher & remediation

Targeted retraining when drift shows up in the data.

How it works

Measured at every step, like everything we do.

1

Needs assessment

We review your current training materials, observe sessions and identify the gaps.

2

Curriculum & delivery

Workshops built on behavioral skills training: instruction, modeling, rehearsal and feedback.

3

Verification

Staff demonstrate competency in real or role-played sessions before sign-off.

4

Maintenance

Spot checks and booster sessions keep skills from drifting.

  • An audit or payer review found documentation gaps.
  • High turnover is eroding consistency across cases.
