About the shop

On Wescove Pl. By appointment.

Red Hair Salon sits on Wescove Pl in West Covina — color, cuts, and pre-event hair and makeup. Small team, by appointment. The kind of place where regulars come back for years and rebook before they leave the chair.

Holly's review put it bluntly: 'Cherry is the only one allowed to touch her hair.' Marlene came in for an event and called it 'comfortable, natural, and beautiful.' Elisa has been a client across five years. The shop runs on these long arcs — not walk-in volume.
