What we're known for

The chilaquiles are our most popular plate, and don't leave without trying the café de olla. It comes in its own little jug.

Tortillas come with your plate, corn or flour. Tell your server which you'd like.

You can dine in, take it to go or have it delivered. We take credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.
