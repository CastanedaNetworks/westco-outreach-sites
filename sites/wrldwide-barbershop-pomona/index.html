About

Built for Pomona, by people from Pomona.

WRLDWIDE BARBERSHOP sits on Indian Hill Blvd, right next door to Tierra Mia Coffee. Five chairs, a deep bench of barbers — Alex, Erik, Chuy — and a regular flow of dads bringing their kids in for their first cut.

Five stars across 443 Google reviews isn't an accident. People come back because the cuts hold, the conversation is good, and nobody walks out unhappy.
