How it works

What a visit looks like.

  1. i.

    Message to book.

    Send Faith a message on Instagram with the day that works and what you want done. She usually gets back to you the same day.

  2. ii.

    Come to the studio.

    Once your deposit is in, she sends over the West Covina address. Show up at your time and the room is already set up and ready for you.

  3. iii.

    Let go for an hour.

    Get on the table and switch off for an hour, or ninety minutes if you booked it. The room stays quiet and it is only ever you in there.

The studio

A private space in West Covina.

Every session happens in Faith's home studio in West Covina. It is a quiet room she set up just for massage, not a spa floor with ten other tables going at once. Because it is appointment only, you get the place to yourself.

You will get the exact address and parking details after your deposit goes through.

Good to know

Before you book.

A few things worth knowing ahead of time so booking is easy.

Hours

Tuesday to Saturday

Open 11am to 10pm, by appointment. Need a time outside that? Early or late bookings are a $30 add-on.

Deposit

$20 to book

A $20 deposit holds your spot and counts toward what you pay ($30 for early or late bookings). Deposits are not refundable.

Running late

15 minute grace

There is a 15 minute grace period if you are running behind. After that the appointment may be canceled.

Respect

A safe space

This is professional, therapeutic massage only. Anything inappropriate ends the session, and you will not be able to book again.
