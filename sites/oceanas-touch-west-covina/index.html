How it works

Three steps. No friction.

  1. i.

    DM to set a time.

    Reach out on Instagram with a day, a window, and what you're looking for. Faith replies same day most of the week.

  2. ii.

    Come to the studio.

    Once your deposit confirms the slot, Faith shares the West Covina address. Arrive at your time — the table, linens, and oils are already set.

  3. iii.

    You unwind.

    One uninterrupted hour, or ninety minutes if you have it — in a space kept calm, private, and set up just for your session.

The studio

A private space in West Covina.

Sessions take place in Faith's private home studio in West Covina — a calm, dedicated room set up for massage, not a busy spa floor. It's appointment-only, so you'll never share the space with anyone else.

The exact address is shared once your deposit confirms the time. Parking and any arrival notes come with your confirmation.

Good to know

Before you book.

A few simple things keep every session smooth — so the hour stays about you, not the logistics.

Hours

Tue – Sat

11 AM to 10 PM, by appointment. Before- or after-hours sessions are available for a $30 fee.

Deposit

$20 to book

A $20 deposit holds your slot ($30 for before/after-hours). It applies toward your service and is non-refundable.

Arrival

15-min grace

Please be ready on time — there's a 15-minute grace period, after which the appointment may be canceled.

Respect

A safe space

This is a strictly professional, therapeutic service. Inappropriate conduct ends the session and bookings.
