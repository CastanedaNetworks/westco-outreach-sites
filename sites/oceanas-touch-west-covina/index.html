About

Faith.

Faith is a massage therapist based in West Covina. She works mobile — driving to your home, setting up a portable table in the room of your choosing, and handling the linens and oils so you don't have to.

Each session is one-on-one. No reception desk, no fluorescent overhead, no rotating therapist. Book once. Or set a standing weekly slot.

Quiet luxury, brought to the room you live in

The best part isn't getting a massage. It's not having to leave to get one.

Treatments

What Faith offers.

Choose a focus when you book — Faith will tailor pressure, technique, and oils to the session. Combinations welcome. Specifics confirmed when you message.

  1. 01

    Swedish

    Long flowing strokes, light to medium pressure. Built for relaxation and circulation — the most-requested session for a reason.

  2. 02

    Deep tissue

    Slower, deeper work into the muscle layers under the surface. For chronic tension in the neck, shoulders, lower back. Tell Faith where it lives.

  3. 03

    Prenatal

    Side-lying setup with bolsters. Pressure and positioning adjusted for second and third trimester. Cleared with your provider.

  4. 04

    Recovery

    Sports and post-workout focus — flushing, light stretching, targeted relief on overworked groups. Best 12–48 hours after training.

  5. 05

    Custom

    Couples sessions, weekly standing slots, occasion bookings (birthday, anniversary, returning from travel). Message Faith with what you have in mind.

How it works

Three steps. No friction.

  1. i.

    DM to set a time.

    Reach out on Instagram with a day, a window, and what you're looking for. Faith replies same day most of the week.

  2. ii.

    Faith arrives.

    With the portable table, fresh linens, oils, and a small speaker if you want music. Setup takes five minutes in any room.

  3. iii.

    You unwind.

    One uninterrupted hour, or ninety minutes if you have it. Faith handles cleanup, leaves the room exactly as she found it.
