About

Twenty years standing between South Florida homeowners and the carrier.

When you file a property damage claim, the insurance company sends their adjuster — a salaried employee whose job is to settle the file for as little as legally possible. A public adjuster works the other side. We represent you, document the loss, prove its value, and negotiate until the settlement matches the damage.

Saskia Cruz has been doing exactly that since 2003. Over 2,000 claims and supplements. Appraisal and umpire experience for disputes that escalate. When the file needs more — attorneys, contractors, engineers — she brings the right people in.

What's covered

Most South Florida claims. Residential and commercial.

01

Water damage

Flood, roof leak, plumbing failure, cast-iron pipe collapse. The most common — and most often underpaid — claim type in Miami-Dade.

02

Fire & smoke

Structural fire, kitchen fire, electrical fire. Smoke damage spreads further than the flames — full documentation matters.

03

Mold

Mold from undetected leaks, humidity, or post-loss conditions. Often denied or undervalued without proper testing and reports.

04

Theft & vandalism

Stolen contents and intentional damage. Documentation of what was lost — and what it actually replaces for — drives the settlement.

05

Commercial property

Loss of business income, building damage, contents. Commercial policies move differently than residential — and so does the negotiation.

06

Appraisal & expert testimony

When a claim escalates — appraisal, umpire, expert testimony. Saskia's been on all three sides of the table.

How it works

Four steps. You don't write a check until step four.

  1. 01

    Free inspection

    Saskia comes to the property, walks the loss, and tells you whether a claim is worth filing. If it isn't, you'll know.

  2. 02

    Documentation

    Photos, scope of loss, contractor estimates, supporting reports. The insurance company will demand all of it — we have it ready.

  3. 03

    Negotiation

    Submission, follow-up with the carrier's adjuster, supplement filings, and counter-offers. This is most of the work.

  4. 04

    Settlement

    When the check arrives, our fee comes out of the recovery — not out of pocket. If there's no recovery, there's no fee.

Why a public adjuster

The carrier's adjuster works for the carrier.

Homeowners almost always under-document their losses. They show the obvious damage. They miss the secondary loss — the mold behind the drywall, the smoke residue in the HVAC, the contents that need cleaning rather than replacement. Then the carrier's adjuster writes the file based on what got shown.

A public adjuster knows what to look for, what to photograph, what to demand. Saskia's been on 2,000+ claims; the patterns are by now obvious. Most clients see a meaningfully larger settlement after she's on the file — and pay nothing for the work until that settlement closes.
