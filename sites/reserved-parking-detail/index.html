Questions

Before you book

Do you really come to me?

Yes. We're mobile, so the detail happens at your home or your work. You don't have to drop the car off anywhere.

How much is a full detail?

The Reset Detail is $400 to $450 for cars and $500 to $550 for trucks and SUVs. Use the builder above to add things like shampoo, headlights or ceramic coating and see your range.

Do you have something smaller than the Reset Detail?

Yes. We have smaller packages and regular maintenance washes. Text us and we'll send the full menu.

Can you fix scratches and cloudy headlights?

Yes. Scratch removal runs $100 to $300 depending on the scratches, and headlight restoration is $150.

Do you do ceramic coating?

Yes, $400 to $600. Text us the vehicle and we'll give you the exact price.

How do I book?

Call or text (626) 290-7644, or message us on Instagram. We're open 7 days a week, 7 AM to 7 PM.
