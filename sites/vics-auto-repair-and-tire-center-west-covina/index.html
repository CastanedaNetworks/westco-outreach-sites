What we work on

If you don't see your problem here, call anyway. If it's something we don't do, Vic will tell you who does.

  • Engine problemsRough running, stalling, noises, loss of power. We find the cause before we replace anything.
  • Check engine lightWe read the code, confirm what's actually wrong and fix it. Oxygen sensors are a common one.
  • Used car inspectionsBuying a used car or van? Bring it by first. We go through it and give you a full rundown of what we find.
  • TiresCall with your tire size, or just tell us what you drive, and we'll tell you what we can get and what it costs.
  • General repair and maintenanceThe everyday work that keeps a car on the road. Call and tell us what it's doing.

We no longer do wheel alignments. If you need one, ask and we'll point you to a local shop we trust.

You'll deal with Vic

Vic is the one who greets you, looks at your car and explains what he found.

Customers say the same thing in different words: he's honest. If a repair isn't worth doing, or it's a small fix, he'll say so. A few have written that he fixed something minor and didn't charge them at all.

If you can, call ahead so we can set a time. If it can't wait, come by and we'll do our best to fit you in.
