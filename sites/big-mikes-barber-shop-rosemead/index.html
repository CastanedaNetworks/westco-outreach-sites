About the shop

We cut hair for men and kids at 7849 Garvey Ave. There's a full crew of barbers, so if you have someone you like, ask for them when you call. If you don't, we'll put you with whoever's free.

You can also message us on Facebook.

Good to know

Appointments and walk-ins
We take both. Appointments go first, so call ahead if you're short on time and we'll tell you what the wait looks like.
Kids
Bring them. We cut kids' hair every day.
Parking
You can park right in front of the shop.
Getting in
The entrance, the parking and the restroom are wheelchair accessible.
