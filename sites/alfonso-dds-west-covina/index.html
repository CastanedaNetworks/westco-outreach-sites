New patients

What to expect at your first visit

  1. 1

    A full exam. We review your health history, take any X-rays you need and look at every tooth and your gums.

  2. 2

    A clear plan. Dr. Alfonso walks you through what he sees and what, if anything, needs attention.

  3. 3

    Costs up front. We go over your insurance and your share before any treatment is scheduled.

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card and a list of your current medications.
