Dental services in West Covina

Most of what your family needs can be done here, in one office, by the same dentist each time.

Preventive care

Exams and digital X-rays, cleanings, deep cleanings for gum health, fluoride and sealants.

Fillings and crowns

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, root canal treatment, dentures and partials.

Cosmetic

Whitening, bonding and veneers. Come in for a consultation and we will show you what is realistic for your teeth.

Extractions

Including teeth that are broken or can't be saved, with clear instructions for the days after.

What to expect at your first visit

  1. 1

    We go over your health history, take the X-rays you need and look at every tooth and your gums.

  2. 2

    Dr. Alfonso shows you what he sees and tells you what needs attention now and what can wait.

  3. 3

    Before anything is scheduled, the front desk goes over your insurance and what your share would be.

Insurance

Paying without insurance
