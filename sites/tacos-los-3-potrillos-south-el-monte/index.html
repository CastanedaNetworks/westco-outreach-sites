The full menu is at the counter. Call ahead and we'll have your order ready to pick up.

Eat here or take it home

There are plenty of tables inside if you want to sit down, and we do a lot of takeout too. We have our own parking lot, a restroom for customers, and the entrance and seating are wheelchair accessible.

We take cash, credit and debit cards, and tap to pay.
