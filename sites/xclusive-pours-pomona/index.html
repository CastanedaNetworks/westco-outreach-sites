How a pour goes

Four steps, one clean result

01

Consult & quote

Call or DM. We look at the space, talk colors and finish, and send a free, honest quote.

02

Grind & prep

Diamond-grind the concrete, fix cracks and profile the slab so the coating bonds for good.

03

Pour & design

The fun part. We lay the base, marble the metallics or broadcast the flake, and shape the look live.

04

Topcoat & cure

A high-build clear seals it glass-hard. Light foot traffic next day, fully cured within the week.
