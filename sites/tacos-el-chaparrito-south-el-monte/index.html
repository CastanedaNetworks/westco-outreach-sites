Two ways to eat here

Inside

It's a small dining room with tables, plus seating outside. Come in for breakfast, lunch or dinner.

At the truck

Our taco truck is parked out front. It's the fastest way to grab tacos or a burrito, and it's there late.

Dine in, take it to go, or get it delivered. We take credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.
