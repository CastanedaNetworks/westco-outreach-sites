When you come in

We bring salsa and tostadas to the table while you look at the menu. Pozole and chilaquiles are what people write about most.

Menudo, every day

We make menudo every day. You can bring your own pot and we'll fill it for you to take home.

There's a parking lot. The seating and restroom are wheelchair accessible.
