§ 04 — Questions

Asked & answered.

How do I place an order? +

DM @baked.bykey with your flavors, quantity, the date you need them, and whether you want pickup or delivery.

Why a deposit? +

It's what locks your batch in for that date. No deposit, no order — and it goes toward your total, so you're not paying extra.

How do I pay? +

Zelle or Apple Pay. Details come through in the DM once your order's confirmed.

Pickup or delivery? +

Both. Pickup is free; delivery is a $10 fee depending on distance around Lancaster and the Antelope Valley.

How far ahead should I order? +

Everything's pre-order and baked to order, so sooner is better — especially for big boxes and events. DM for the next available batch.

Custom orders or events? +

Yes — birthdays, parties, dessert tables. Send the date and headcount and Key will put a box together for you.
