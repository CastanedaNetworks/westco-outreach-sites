§ 04 — FAQ

Asked & answered.

Q How do I place an order? +
DM @baked.bykey on Instagram with your flavors, quantity, the date you need them, and whether you want pickup or delivery.
Q Do you require a deposit? +
Yes — a deposit is what locks your batch in. No deposit, no order. It goes toward your total and reserves your spot for that date.
Q How do I pay? +
Zelle or Apple Pay. Payment details are sent in the DM once your order is confirmed.
Q Do you deliver, or is it pickup? +
Both. Pickup is available, or add delivery for a $10 fee that depends on distance around Lancaster and the Antelope Valley.
Q How far in advance should I order? +
Everything is pre-order and baked to order, so the sooner the better — especially for bigger boxes. DM for the next available batch date.
Q Can you do custom orders or events? +
DM with what you have in mind — birthdays, parties, dessert tables. Send the date and headcount and Key will put a box together for you.
