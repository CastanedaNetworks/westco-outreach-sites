About

A grooming salon dogs actually like.

serenity pet grooming sits on Phillips Blvd in Pomona. Small, friendly staff, easy parking, and a real talent for handling nervous dogs.

Most reviews say the same thing in different words — the staff is gentle, the prices are fair, and your dog comes out looking great without the stress signals you'd see at a bigger chain.
