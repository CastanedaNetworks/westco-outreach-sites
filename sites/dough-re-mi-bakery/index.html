Hi, I'm Katie.

Dough Re Mi started because I missed the sourdough I grew up eating in San Francisco. Bread bowls from Boudin in the Macy's basement café and the warm sourdough at House of Prime Rib are still the standard I compare everything to.

What started as chasing that SF tang slowly turned into something I genuinely love. Baking is how I unwind after my 9 to 5 and mom life, and somewhere along the way I started bringing in the Filipino flavors I grew up with too.

Thank you for being here. I can't wait to bake for you.
