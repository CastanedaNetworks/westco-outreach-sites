After care

Keep the sparkle.

A few easy rules. Follow them and your gem stays bonded comfortably for months — most clients get well past a year before they're ready for a refresh.

  1. 01

    No eating or drinking for 2 hours.

    Let the adhesive fully set before you put anything on it.

  2. 02

    No hot drinks for 24 hours.

    Coffee, tea, soup — let it cool first for the first day.

  3. 03

    Brush gently — go around the gem.

    Soft-bristle brush. Don't scrub directly over it.

  4. 04

    Don't pick or floss-tug at it.

    Floss next to it, not through it. Picking is the #1 reason gems pop early.

  5. 05

    Skip biting hard objects.

    Ice, pen caps, fingernails. Treat the gem like a tiny piece of jewelry — because it is.

  6. 06

    Gem fell off? DM us.

    If it pops within the first 7 days, the re-set is on the house.

Questions

The FAQ.

Does it hurt? +

No. The application is completely painless — no needles, no drilling, no numbing. We use a dental-grade adhesive that bonds to the enamel surface, the same kind of resin orthodontists use.

Will it damage my teeth? +

No drilling, no scraping, no damage to enamel. The gem sits on the surface of the tooth and can be removed any time with no trace left behind.

How long does it last? +

Six to twelve months on average, often longer with good after-care. Many of our regulars keep theirs in for a year+ before they're ready for a change.

What if it falls off? +

If a gem comes off within 7 days of placement, the re-set is free. After that we'll re-set at a small re-application fee — just DM us a photo.

Can I do a group session? +

Absolutely — and it's our favorite kind of booking. Birthdays, bachelorettes, girls' nights. Three or more and travel is on us. DM us with how many and where.

Can I pick custom colors? +

Yes. The case travels with us — clear, gold, rose, red, blue, green, black, multicolor. Send a reference pic of any combo you want and we'll match it.

How do I book? +

Instagram DM at @thegemlab.la. Include design, date window, and your zip — we'll confirm the rest.
