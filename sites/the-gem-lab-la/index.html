§ 04 — After care

Keep the sparkle.

Six small rules. Follow them and the gem stays bonded comfortably — most of our regulars get well past a year before they swap.

Mara · disco ball

Jess · cherry set

  1. 01

    No eating or drinking for 2 hours.

    Let the adhesive set fully before you put anything on it.

  2. 02

    No hot drinks for 24 hours.

    Coffee, tea, soup — let them cool first the first day.

  3. 03

    Brush gently. Go around, not over.

    Soft-bristle. Don't scrub directly on the gem.

  4. 04

    Don't pick. Don't floss-tug.

    Floss next to it, not through it. Picking is the #1 reason gems pop early.

  5. 05

    Skip biting hard objects.

    Ice, pen caps, fingernails. Treat it like a tiny piece of jewelry — it is.

  6. 06

    Gem fell off in the first 7 days?

    DM us a photo. The re-set is on the house.

§ 06 — FAQ

Asked & answered.

Q Does it hurt? +
No. Completely painless — no needles, no numbing, no drilling. We use a dental-grade adhesive that bonds to the enamel surface, the same kind of resin orthodontists use.
Q Will it damage my teeth? +
No drilling, no scraping, no damage to enamel. The gem sits on the surface of the tooth and can be removed any time with zero trace left behind.
Q How long does it last? +
Six to twelve months on average, often longer with good after-care. Many of our regulars keep theirs in for a year+ before they're ready for a change.
Q What if it falls off? +
Within 7 days of placement, the re-set is free. After that we'll re-set at a small re-application fee. Just DM us a photo.
Q Can I do a group session? +
Absolutely — birthdays, bachelorettes, girls' nights. Three or more and travel is on us. DM with how many and where and we'll send a slot.
Q Can I pick custom colors? +
Yes. The case travels with us — clear, gold, rose, red, blue, green, black, multicolor. Send a reference pic of any combo you want and we'll match it.
Q How do I book? +
DM us at @thegemlab.la. Include design, date window, and your zip — we'll confirm the rest the same day.
