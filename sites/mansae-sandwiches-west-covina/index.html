THE STORY

FROM A TENT IN THE 626

Mansae started as a tent, a flat top griddle and a stack of milk bread at San Gabriel Valley night markets. The sandwich is our take on gilgeori toast, the egg toast sold on street corners all over Korea, built bigger and messier the way a night market deserves.

If you have stood in our line at 626 Night Market, thank you for real. If you are new, start with the bulgogi and grab napkins. More than one.
