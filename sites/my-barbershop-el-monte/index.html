About

Same chair, same hands, 30–40 fresh cuts a day.

My BarberShop on Tyler Ave is exactly what it sounds like — one barber, Ruben, who's been keeping the SGV looking sharp for years. No second chair, no extra hands, no rushing through to clear a queue.
