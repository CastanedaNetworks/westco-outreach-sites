01The shop

Round 01 · The shop

A family chair, held twice.

SN Barbershop has been at 4705 Peck Road in El Monte since 1997. The founder cut hair there for years before passing the chair to his son. The shop stayed open. The clippers kept running. The customers kept coming back.

It is a small family-shop story that doesn't get marketed because it doesn't have to be. The proof is in the chair: razor fades, blends, tapers, combovers, flattops, beards. Cash only. ATM at the front. Walk-in any day except Sunday — appointments take over the weekend.

The reviews are forgiving even of bad days. They are not forgiving of much else. Carlos came back a third time. Gabriela called the place clean and the staff really nice. Jose, after one walk-in, called it “the best haircut I've ever gotten.”

“I drive 3 hours every 3 weeks to get here. So for me, well worth it.” — Art Mendoza · Google review
